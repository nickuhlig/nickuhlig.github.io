**Update (2026):** one of the earliest and worst offenders at wrapping itself in the Canadian flag while making things overseas. Most apparel has been made abroad since about 2006; only leather goods are still made at its Toronto leather factory ([Retail Insider](https://retail-insider.com/retail-insider/2025/02/roots-ceo-discusses-canadian-pride-manufacturing-and-growth/)). Its "Made in Canada" page still offers a made-in-Canada collection, though it's now titled "Made for Canada" ([site](https://www.roots.com/ca/en/made-in-canada.html)). Controlled by US private equity firm Searchlight Capital since 2015.

Leather products possibly made in Toronto, ON. Only boots, belts, wallets, bags, and other leather goods are made in Canada as far as I know.
