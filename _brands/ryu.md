**Update (2026):** ryu.com is now a parked domain. RYU closed its stores in 2022 and moved its headquarters to Toronto ([Retail Insider](https://retail-insider.com/retail-insider/2022/02/vancouver-based-ryu-apparel-to-shut-all-but-1-storefront/)); there has been no news since, and the brand appears to have closed.

Some items made in Vancouver, BC. Technical athletic wear for men and women. Some sweats and core items were made in Vancouver or elsewhere in Canada; much of the stretch gear, women's styles, bags, and outerwear were imported.
