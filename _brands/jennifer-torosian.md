**Update (2026):** still "proudly made in Canada" from European fabrics ([site](https://www.jennifertorosian.com/)).

Made in Toronto, ON. Women's clothing with a mix of minimalist monochrome garments and patterned fabrics. The line incorporates a lot of technical fabrics into otherwise everyday items. Founded in 2012 by Jennifer Torosian and her partner Rae Ali, both former employees of Holt Renfrew.
