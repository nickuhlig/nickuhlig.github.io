**Update (2026):** le Château filed for creditor protection in 2023 and has since closed all its stores.

Headquartered in Ville St.-Laurent, QC. Some items made in Canada, most are not. Not sure where exactly the MiC stuff is actually manufactured. Men's and women's formal-ish clothing. Bit of a younger look to most of their clothing, and generally they put more emphasis on their women's line. Found in malls everywhere.
