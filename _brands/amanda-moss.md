**Update (2026):** still going: "Made with Love in Canada" ([site](https://www.amandamoss.ca/)).

Made in Montréal, QC. Casual to business-casual dresses and tops for women. Patterns are fairly simple, and the cuts on some of the more recent pieces are very minimalist, again espousing a vaguely Japanese aesthetic. Others are more typical, but generally design flourishes, if present at all, are very understated, while the solid colours tend to be fairly vibrant.
