**Update (2026):** in 2018 Bummis and its sister brand Funky Fluff announced they were closing "for an undetermined period of time", and the domain now redirects to an unrelated site, and the brand appears to have closed. ([Cloth Diaper Kids](https://clothdiaperkids.com/blogs/news/bummis-funky-fluff-closing))

Made in Montréal, QC. Cloth diapers and a variety of accessories for babies and those who care for them, most of which were made in Canada from fabrics milled in North America.
