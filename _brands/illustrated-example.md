**Update (2026):** the label is defunct; its site no longer loads and the last coverage is from 2017 ([Hypebeast](https://hypebeast.com/2017/11/livestock-illustrated-example-fall-2017-editorial)).

Made in Vancouver, BC and other locations within Canada. Clothing line owned by the same folks as Livestock. Streetwear with a techwear influence, featuring a lot of waterproof and breathable fabrics and an emphasis on functionality. Vaguely similar in aesthetic to Arc'teryx Veilance and [wings + horns](#brand-wings-and-horns), but more accessibly priced. Many of their fabrics came from Japan, some from Ontario.
