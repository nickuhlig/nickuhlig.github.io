**Update (2026):** "Proudly Canadian", with its manufacturing facility in Burnaby. It makes its own Rough Dress and Tuff Athletics labels, licensed brands like Avia and Ellen Tracy, and private label for retailers such as Mark's ([site](https://www.coapparel.ca/)).

Made in Burnaby, BC. Menswear and womenswear. Parent company that manufactures under several brands, both created by them and acquired from other companies, spanning regular apparel to winter-oriented outerwear. Their own label "Rough Dress" is, I believe, sold at Costco. They also have a label called "Tuff Athletics" that sells, as you guessed, athletic wear. Their website suggests that they do some manufacturing overseas, but this could be in reference to the sourcing of their fabrics, as opposed to the actual construction of garments.
