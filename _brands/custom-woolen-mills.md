**Update (2026):** still running the mill in Carstairs, where its wool bedding is "expertly crafted at our mill" ([site](https://www.customwoolenmills.com/)).

Made in Carstairs, AB. A family-run woolen mill that has been operating for a few generations. They primarily sell yarn and wool, but also make socks, blankets, and bedding. They sell kits to make your own garments as well, if you're one of the hard-core DIY knitting types.
