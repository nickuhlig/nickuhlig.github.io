**Update (2026):** the clothing line is gone; sunjalink.com is now Sunja Link Body Shoppe, a Vancouver spa and beauty shop.

Made in Vancouver, BC. Simple dresses for women, made from Italian and Japanese fabrics, with a very natural look: few details and loose fits that make them seem free-moving and easy to wear. Solid colours generally, with lookbooks that reinforced the association with natural colours and textures.
