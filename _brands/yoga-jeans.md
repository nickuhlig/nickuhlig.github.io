**Update (2026):** Second Jeans now goes by Yoga Jeans. It still runs its own 52,000 sq ft factory in the Beauce with 60+ workers, but since COVID it has also used factories abroad ([their sustainability page](https://yogajeans.ca/pages/sustainability)), and its site now has a separate "Made in Canada Collection". Check the label of a given pair.

Made in Beauce, QC. Known for their "yoga jeans" which have a lot of stretch and are apparently quite comfortable. A favourite of many women I know. They also make some other women's garments, but free moving jeans are the mainstay. Direct quote from a lady I know who tried their jeans: "They make my bum look like some sort of exotic fruit."
