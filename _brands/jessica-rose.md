**Update (2026):** the shop is gone. jessicarosedesign.com is now a design portfolio whose latest project is from 2018, so the clothing line appears to have ended. Earlier coverage: [blogTO](https://www.blogto.com/fashion/jessica-rose-toronto/).

Made in Toronto, ON. Founded in 2009. Ladies' clothing, primarily dresses in a number of different cuts and patterns. Range goes from decidedly casual to fairly professional, though nothing crazy formal. Emphasis on versatility between climates, packability for those who travel, and freedom of movement.
