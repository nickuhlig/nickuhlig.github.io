**Update (2026):** signature collections are made at its Winnipeg headquarters, but growing sales led it to move much production to China and Vietnam; in 2017 at least 20% was made in Winnipeg ([CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/lite/story/1.3197893)).

About 20% made in Winnipeg, MB. Métis-owned maker of mukluks, moccasins, and storyboots decorated by Indigenous artisans, many on a custom Vibram sole designed by Cree artist Heather Endall.
