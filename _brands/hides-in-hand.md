**Update (2026):** "Made in Canada, made from Canada for over 25 years" ([site](https://www.hidesinhand.com/)).

Made in Rockwood, ON, not far from Guelph. Footwear, gloves, hats, bags, and accessories, including moccasins in moose, deer, and bison hide. Once called Marzo Glove, a holdover from when the area around Acton was known for leather production, with an unusual emphasis on leather sourced and tanned in Canada.
