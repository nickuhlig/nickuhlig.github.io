**Update (2026):** "Made in Montreal since 2012" ([site](https://www.woolfell.net/)).

Made in Montréal, QC. Primarily bags for city dwellers. They use a lot of leather for their more fashion-oriented bags, but also make a range of highly functional and waterproof duffels, backpacks, and shoulder bags. They also make some apparel and knick-knacks like coasters, keychains, and cellphone cases.
