**Update (2026):** still sewing everything in-house at its Kitchener plant, which knits its own fabric from North American fibres ([Rugby Ontario](https://sirc.ca/?p=47860), [Support Ontario Made](https://supportontariomade.ca/fabricant/kitchener/barbarian-sports-wear-inc)).

Made in Kitchener, ON. As the name implies, they make rugby-focused clothing, including more casual cotton rugby shirts (those stripy, collared ones) as well as more modern sportswear for people who actually play rugby. They also make socks, shorts, and a few other items.
