**Update (2026):** "89% of our skincare products are proudly made in Canada", with its manufacturing, marketing, and innovation teams based in Canada ([site](https://www.marcelle.com/)).

Some products made in Montréal, QC. While originally founded in 1874 in Chicago, IL, Marcelle was purchased in 1973 by Canadian distributor Victor Cape, and the head office moved to Montréal, where much of their formulation and manufacturing occurs to this day. Owner of [Watier](#brand-watier).
