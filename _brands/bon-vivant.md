**Update (2026):** Bon Vivant is defunct; its old domain is now an unrelated blog.

Made in Montréal, QC. Menswear. In-house brand of a shop that sells a lot of the Canadian-made fashions here. They make button-down shirts, some pants and shorts, and a few bags. Style is sort of a toss-up between Naked & Famous and Muttonhead. They have shirts in nice solid colours, as well as some more interesting patterns, and in a range of fabrics including oxford cloth, linen, and gingham. Most of their fabrics are produced in Japan and the garments are made here.
