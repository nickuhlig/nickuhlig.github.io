**Update (2026):** the store no longer has the made-in-Canada filter it had in 2019, and says nothing about where most items are made. Its "Made in Nova Scotia" collection, made with Stanfield's in Truro, is the confirmed Canadian-made part ([CTV](https://www.ctvnews.ca/atlantic/article/two-iconic-clothing-companies-team-up-to-create-made-in-nova-scotia-collection/)).

Some items made in Canada, not sure where. Rumour has it that this company occasionally cuts the tags off of their items that are made overseas in order to obscure their supply chain. Proceed with caution. Brand is based out of Halifax, NS. Sweats, tees, some accessories such as hats and bags. Leaning towards streetwear in their aesthetic. Of 156 items in their online store, 26 (17%) are made here (you can filter for it in their webstore). The made in Canada items are predominantly sweats and t-shirts.
