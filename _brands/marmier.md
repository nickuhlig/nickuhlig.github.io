**Update (2026):** Marmier is Betina Lou's menswear house brand; its old site now redirects to Betina Lou's, which has a menswear section but no longer says where things are made.

Made in Montréal, QC. No-nonsense contemporary menswear, including short- and long-sleeved button downs, tees, sweats, shorts, and trousers. The button downs incorporate a few very simple patterns, while all other garments are solid colours primarily in earth tones. They also feature a number of accessories, including belts, tuques, and scarves.
