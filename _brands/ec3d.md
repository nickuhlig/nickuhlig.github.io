**Update (2026):** still active. Its site makes no made-in claim, but a directory says every product is made in Québec from mostly North American materials ([Made in CA](https://madeinca.ca/?p=5192)).

Made in Montréal, QC. Founded by Gilbert and Daniel Ayoub, who also co-founded Gildan. EC3D makes compression garments for men and women, from socks to tights and shirts.
