Made in Montréal, QC. Founded by the same people who founded Gildan (Gilbert and Daniel Ayoub, who owned the factory bought by the Chamandy family in 1984 which grew to become the Gildan we know today). EC3D makes compression garments for men and women, ranging from socks to tights and shirts. Not all of their items are made in Canada, but their website says they are all made in North America.
