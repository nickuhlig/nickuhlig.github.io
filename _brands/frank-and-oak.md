**Update (2026):** Frank and Oak went through creditor protection in 2020 (bought by New York's Unified Commerce Group) and again in early 2025, closed most of its stores, and [its brand was sold to Lamour Group and Thread Collective](https://fashionunited.uk/news/business/frank-and-oak-ip-to-be-sold-to-lamour-group-and-thread-collective/2025042581291). Very few, if any, of its items are made in Canada; it was never especially a made-in-Canada brand.

A few items made in Canada, not sure where. This brand used to be known primarily for their simple menswear and home try-on service, as well as their subscription (previously called the Hunt Club). While they previously only made clothing overseas, Frank and Oak has begun doing some of their manufacturing in Canada, and they now have a women's line. Made in Canada pieces include denim (for under $100), and formerly some sweatshirts and tees.
