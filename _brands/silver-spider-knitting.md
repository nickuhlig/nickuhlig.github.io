**Update (2026):** still operating as a Toronto knitting mill ("Support Canadian Made today!") making toques, sweaters, mittens and scarves ([site](http://www.silverspider.ca/)).

Made in North York (Toronto), ON. Silver Spider is a daughter company of KNP Headwear. They make knitwear ranging from scarves to tuques and sweaters, as well as offering printing, embroidery, and appliqué embellishments to their blanks for private labels.
