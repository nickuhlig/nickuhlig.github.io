**Update (2026):** kazzclothing.com no longer loads, and I couldn't find any current trace of the brand.

Made in Toronto, ON. Another women's label focused on the minimalist aesthetic. Earth tones are dominant, and garments have very few details, with the emphasis on overall shape more than ornaments or small flourishes. Onesies, pants, tops, and dresses, as well as some more tomboy-ish items including loose-fitting button-down shirts.
