**Update (2026):** the company says "the majority of our product is proudly manufactured in Canada and Europe" ([site](https://www.josephribkoff.com/)). Production is still largely at its Dorval (Montréal) headquarters, with a facility in Bulgaria since 2019 ([Canadian Manufacturing](https://www.canadianmanufacturing.com/manufacturing/joseph-ribkoff-announces-investments-from-novacap-fonds-de-solidarite-ftq-and-dna-partners-299274/), [Retail Insider](https://retail-insider.com/retail-insider/2026/03/joseph-ribkoff-expands-globally-under-new-ceo/)).

Mostly made in Montréal, QC. Joseph Ribkoff is a Montréal-based designer of women's workwear who began with mostly tops and dresses. Pieces tend to be made of stretchier material and colourful patterns geared toward older professionals. A store locator is available on the website.
