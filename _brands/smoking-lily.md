**Update (2026):** "our small team designs, screenprints, cuts and sews everything in our harbour front studio" in Victoria ([site](https://www.smokinglily.com/)).

Made in Victoria, BC. Women's wear primarily, but also a few kids' designs and a couple of t-shirts for men. All sewn and silk-screened in Victoria. The women's line encompasses blouses, tees, skirts, dresses, and some accessories like shoulder bags. Fairly playful and colourful designs as a rule, with the in-house silk-screening much in evidence.
