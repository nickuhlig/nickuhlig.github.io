**Update (2026):** the website has expired, and the brand appears to have closed. Press coverage places it in designer Julia Rechel's East Vancouver studio, where each piece was made by hand, rather than in Montréal as the 2019 guide said ([Vancouver Is Awesome](https://www.vancouverisawesome.com/courier-archive/living/vancouver-designer-crafts-lingerie-for-the-masses-3029584)).

Made in Vancouver, BC. Lingerie label founded by Yukon-born designer Julia Rechel, a one-woman operation offering an alternative to fast-fashion lingerie. Ellesmere drew inspiration from the Northern Lights and Canada's north in crafting colourful and unique lingerie.
