**Update (2026):** "Designed in Montreal for more than 50 years, with 30% of our clothing made locally"; its site has a Made in Canada category ([site](https://www.tristanstyle.com/en/)). Its blazers and jackets are made by [Vêtements Cookshire](#brand-vetements-cookshire).

Headquartered in Montréal, QC. Alternatively (or formerly?) named Tristan & Iseut. Broad array of clothing and accessories for men and women. Some items made in Canada, but not sure where the MiC stuff is actually manufactured. One manufacturing facility is located in Cookshire, QC, but this location only makes blazers and jackets.
