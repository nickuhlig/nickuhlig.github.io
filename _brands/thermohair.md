**Update (2026):** still knitting and finishing its socks in Ontario, from imported South African mohair ([madeinca.ca](https://madeinca.ca/footwear-accessories-thermohair/)).

Made in South Mountain, ON. Socks made from kid mohair (a specific type of goat wool), a soft but highly durable natural fibre. They make a few different types of socks, as well as throws, mittens, and a few other odds and ends.
