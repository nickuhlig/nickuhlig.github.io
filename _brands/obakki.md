**Update (2026):** Obakki no longer makes clothing. Founder Treana Peake left fashion, and [obakki.com](https://obakki.com/) now sells home decor from artisans around the world ([Wallpaper](https://www.wallpaper.com/design-interiors/obakki-homeware-collection-made-in-morocco)).

Made in Vancouver, BC. Founded in 2005 by Treana Peake. Elegant and simple women's clothing from skirts to jackets and scarves. Obakki's revenues support the operating and administrative costs of its philanthropic counterpart, the [Obakki Foundation](https://obakkifoundation.org/), which works to provide better access to drinking water and education in Africa.
