Not sure where made, probably Victoria or somewhere on Vancouver Island. Simple items like henleys, tank tops, sweats, and tees for men and women. Most of their clothes use bamboo fiber, and they aim to manufacture clothing as sustainably as possible. They donate 10% of their profits to the Ancient Forest Alliance.
