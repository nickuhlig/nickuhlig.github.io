**Update (2026):** the skinnysweats.com domain is for sale, and the brand appears to have closed.

Made in Toronto, ON. Like [Muttonhead](#brand-mtn-head), a brand that openly espoused the "slow design" principle, a response to fast fashion. Founded by Adrienne Butikofer. Mostly designed with women in mind, with a few unisex items such as t-shirts and sweatshirts. Decidedly casual, but with enough flair to elevate them above the "crushing a pint of B&J while watching Bridesmaids for the tenth time" echelon.
