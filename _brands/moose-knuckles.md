**Update (2026):** core coats are made in Winnipeg, with some items made overseas (in 2017 the company said about 75% was made in Canada). After a 2016 Competition Bureau challenge, it agreed to make clearer that some parkas use both Canadian and imported components ([Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/2665020/moose-knuckles-accused-of-deceptive-made-in-canada-claims), [Made in CA](https://madeinca.ca/?p=938)).

Some items made in Winnipeg, MB. Conspicuously branded parkas for the Canadian winter. Proceed with caution and read the tags.
