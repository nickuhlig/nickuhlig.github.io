**Update (2026):** nearly every suit in its shop is listed as "Made in Canada" ([site](https://www.bethrichards.com/)).

Made in Vancouver, BC. Women's swimwear (one-pieces and two-pieces) in Italian stretch fabric and occasionally recycled materials. They also had a line called the CENSOR project, the proceeds of which went to Planned Parenthood and the Gloria Steinem Foundation.
