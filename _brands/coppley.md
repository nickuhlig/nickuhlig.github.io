**Update (2026):** "Creating custom tailored garments for gentlemen since 1883. Proudly Canadian Made" ([site](https://www.coppley.com/)). Its Hamilton factory on MacNab St. makes around 1,000 suits a week ([Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/life/fashion-and-beauty/fashion/the-fabric-of-a-city-hamilton-exhibit-explored-coppleys-impact-on-menswear/article35157065/)).

Made in Hamilton, ON. Men's suits and shirts at similar prices to brands like [Samuelsohn](#brand-samuelsohn). Their company history makes some claims to a "family crest", despite this representing incorrect nomenclature, and the fact that such a thing does not exist in Canadian or British heraldry (coats of arms belong to individuals, not families, and are rarely made hereditary).
