**Update (2026):** the online shop is closed, and the last coverage of the brand is from 2018, and the brand appears to have closed. ([Ottawa Business Journal](https://obj.ca/ottawa-clothing-startup-tackling-sport-hijab-and-turban-market))

Made in Ottawa, ON. Thawrih made headgear for Muslims and Sikhs (including hijabs, turbans, and patkas) and a few tees and tanks. All items were made with athleticism in mind, to allow observant Muslims and Sikhs to still get their swell on at the gym. Items were made primarily by newcomers to Canada, including recently arrived Syrian refugees.
