**Update (2026):** Gilmour Clothing has closed; its site says it "has taken a retirement break".

Made in Vancouver, BC. Active- and casual-wear for women. They have a wide product range, from true athletic wear to more dressed-up items, and with a large selection of patterns and cuts. Many of their garments are made using bamboo-based fabrics, like other lines mentioned here.
