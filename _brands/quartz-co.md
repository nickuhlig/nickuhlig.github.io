**Update (2026):** formerly Quartz Nature; bought and renamed by three brothers in 2015, who later bought their own factory. Its parkas and winter jackets are made in Québec ([Altitude Sports](https://www.altitude-sports.com/blog/quartz-co-s-three-brothers-on-purchasing-their-own-factory-and-what-the-future-holds), [Made in CA](https://madeinca.ca/?p=527)).

Made in Saint-Hyacinthe, QC. Founded in 1997, Quartz made parkas and only parkas, all crafted for harsh winters with fur-lined hoods and lots of insulation.
