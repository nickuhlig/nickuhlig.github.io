**Update (2026):** Horses Atelier [closed in 2026](https://www.ctvnews.ca/business/article/end-of-an-era-canadian-clothing-brand-horses-atelier-to-close-after-14-years/) after 14 years; everything had been sewn within a mile of its Toronto studio.

Made in Toronto, ON. Ludicrously expensive women's fashions, ranging from "smoking dresses" (an interesting and intriguing response to the smoking jacket) to obis (the thick, heavy sashes worn to hold kimonos in place).
