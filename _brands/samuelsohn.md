**Update (2026):** "each suit is meticulously hand-crafted in Montreal" ([Harry Rosen](https://www.harryrosen.com/en/editorial/samuelsohn-100-years-of-canadian-made-heritage)). Its parent group also owns Hickey Freeman (bought in 2020, with some production moved to Montréal), [Lipson](#brand-lipson-shirtmakers) and [JP Tilford](#brand-jp-tilford) ([WWD](https://www.pressreader.com/usa/wwd-digital-daily/20200206/281586652590750)).

Made in Montréal, QC. Founded by an emigré from New York in 1923. High-quality suits and shirts, ranging from classic to modern, rakish looks.
