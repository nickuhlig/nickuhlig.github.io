**Update (2026):** Respecterre appears to be defunct; its website is gone and there's no other trace of it.

Made in Ham-Nord, QC. Respecterre is manufactured in an Eco-Village, the largest of its kind in Quebec, known as la Cité écologique. They make a variety of active and lifestyle clothing for men and women, ranging from yoga-focused clothing to underwear and outerwear. Their online shop also sells products by a few other brands.
