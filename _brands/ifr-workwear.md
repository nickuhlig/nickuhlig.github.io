**Update (2026):** acquired by Irish workwear company Portwest in 2023 ([CCIB](https://www.ccib.ca/?p=1251)). Red Deer is now headquarters and distribution; garments are made at the company's own plant in Mexico ([SPI](https://www.spi.com/coveralls-avenger-long-sleeves-reflective-stripes-vcoe37)).

Formerly made in Red Deer, AB. Fire-resistant workwear in a variety of fabrics mostly sourced from North America, for summer and winter, plus high-vis.
