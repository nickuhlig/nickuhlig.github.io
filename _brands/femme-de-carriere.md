**Update (2026):** still going, but its site doesn't say where things are made; a directory says "the majority of their clothing is made in Canada" ([Made in CA](https://madeinca.ca/?p=2711)).

Likely made in QC, not sure where. Some made in Canada lines, similar to [Tristan](#brand-tristan) and le Château. Created in 1979, specializes in women's workwear. Suits, standalone pieces, skirts, and dresses. It's a grab bag - many pieces are for the older crowd, and many pieces are for the mid-20's just starting out with her career crowd.
