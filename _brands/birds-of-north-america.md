**Update (2026):** closed its Toronto shop in January 2024 ([blogTO](https://www.blogto.com/fashion_style/2024/02/birds-of-north-america-toronto-closed/)) but carries on online; still "made in Canada", sewn in Montréal ([site](https://birdsofnorthamerica.com/)).

Made in Toronto, ON. Founded by Victoria, BC native Hayley Gibson in 2007. Primarily dresses, but in the past they have also made onesies, as well as separate pants and blouse combos. The garments have a more youthful feel to them, frequently using graphic prints and some of them having old-timey elements like collars and lots of buttons on dresses. Some garments are starker in appearance, with a sort of 1970s vibe to them.
