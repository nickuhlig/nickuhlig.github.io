**Update (2026):** "all Skoah products are locally crafted in Vancouver", now sold through 11 facial bars in Canada and the US ([site](https://www.skoah.com/)).

Made in Vancouver, BC. All-natural skin care and makeup products. Originally founded in 2001 as a spa in Vancouver's Yaletown district, with the tagline "No whale music, no bubbling cherubs, no pretentious attitudes." Their substantial product line includes shampoos, shave products, lotions, makeups, and more.
