**Update (2026):** still Montréal-based, with denim "cut and sewn in Canada" from Japanese selvedge, and accessories made in Canada. A separate "Made in Japan" collection is made abroad ([site](https://nakedandfamousdenim.com/)).

Made in Montréal, QC. Founded by a rather interesting fellow named Brandon Svarc. Known previously for their use of weird fabrics (like glow-in-the-dark and mint scratch-and-sniff denim...seriously) and for making [the world's heaviest denim jeans](http://www.heddels.com/2012/03/creating-a-monster-naked-famous-32-oz-raw-denim/). Primarily men's raw/selvedge denim, and some apparel (including sweaters and button-downs) but they've also started making ladies' denim in a couple of fits. They also collaborated with an increasingly popular menswear company called Frank & Oak for a few pieces back in 2016/2017. Frank & Oak do not typically manufacture in Canada, unfortunately. N&F have shifted recently from the "weird" denim styles they used to be known for into a more refined and "serious" label, targeting denim-heads around the world. Online shop is located at Tate & Yoko, whose prices are very unfortunately all in US dollars, though there's a 20% discount for Canadian customers to offset the difference.
