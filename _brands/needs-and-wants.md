**Update (2026):** Needs&Wants is defunct.

Made in Toronto, ON. Forward-thinking men's apparel, including jackets and flannel button-downs with unique silhouettes and patterns. One of their trademarks is a pea coat or coach's jacket with each sleeve being a different colour or material (wool on one side and leather on the other, for instance).
