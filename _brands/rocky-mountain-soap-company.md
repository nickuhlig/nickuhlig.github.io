**Update (2026):** "Made in the Canadian Rockies with natural ingredients" ([site](https://www.rockymountainsoap.com/)).

Made in Canmore, AB. Naturally formulated soaps and skin care products, ranging from shampoos to bath salts to deodorants to lotions, and everything in between. They have a "green list" and a "red list", comprising ingredients that can and cannot enter their product lines, respectively. The company was purchased in 2000 by Karina Birch and Cam Baty, and has expanded significantly since then.
