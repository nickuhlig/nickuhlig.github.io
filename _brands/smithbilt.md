**Update (2026):** still making "handcrafted, iconic hats like the Calgary White Hat" ([site](https://smithbilthats.com/)).

Made in Calgary, AB. A hat-making company started by a Russian émigré in 1919. While they make quite a variety of men's and women's hats, their bread and butter is almost certainly the white cowboy hat favoured by folks at the Calgary Stampede.
