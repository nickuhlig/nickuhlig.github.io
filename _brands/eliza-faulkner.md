**Update (2026):** still going; "we make everything in Montreal", in and around her studio in the Chabanel garment district ([Style.ca](https://style.ca/designer-profile-eliza-faulkner/)).

Made in Montréal, QC. Eponymous label founded by former Vancouver Island resident and graduate of Central St. Martins College of Art & Design. Women's apparel that mixes grown-up garments with almost child-like design features, clean lines, and a playful, relaxed feel.
