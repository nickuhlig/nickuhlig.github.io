**Update (2026):** now at emkeveryday.com: "Proudly designed & made in Canada" ([site](https://emkeveryday.com/)).

Made in Winnipeg, MB. Founded in the early 2000s by Erin May Kembel (hence the name). A broad and very diverse line of contemporary women's clothing covering everything from jackets to kimonos and shawls. Prices are very reasonable and style is reasonably diverse while still feeling modern. Lots of solid colours and simple cuts. The website suggests that nearly any garment can be customized at no extra cost, which is a pretty incredible service to offer free of charge.
