**Update (2026):** now based in Magog. The site wouldn't load for me, but sources say the line is made entirely in Québec ([McCord Stewart Museum](https://encyclomodeqc.musee-mccord-stewart.ca/en/entry/message-factory)).

Made in Québec. Launched in 2005 by Julie Rochefort, Message Factory makes a wide range of women's clothing, usually simple garments in solid colours, often with graphics and messages on them. All fabrics are OEKO-TEX® compliant and most are knitted in Canada. Screen printing likewise uses OEKO-TEX® 100 compliant inks. They also have a program that upcycles old adult garments into children's clothing.
