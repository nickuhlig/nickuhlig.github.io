**Update (2026):** still making toques and hats in Canada, but the shop now has a separate "Made in Nepal" section, and roughly a quarter of its products are made in Nepal ([site](https://amblermw.com/)).

Made in Vancouver, BC. The company was originally founded in Canmore, AB by Peter Ambler. Since his death in 2004, it has been owned by a husband-wife duo in Nelson, BC. Makers of hats, from tuques to truckers. Lots have graphic prints on them, but they also make plainer ones in a variety of colours.
