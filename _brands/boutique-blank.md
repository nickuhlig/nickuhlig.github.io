**Update (2026):** the shop is still running, but its site no longer says where things are made; older coverage describes Blank as made entirely in Quebec ([Springwise](https://springwise.com/uncategorized/still_made_in_quebec)).

Made in Montréal, QC, and environs. Casual wear for adults and children. Men's fashions consist mostly of tees, long-sleeves and sweats, while the women's line also extends to skirts and dresses along with other casual wear. They have a few items for babies and some accessories as well (bag, watch cap, etc.). They are the sister brand of Ionik (see the athletic & sportswear section).
