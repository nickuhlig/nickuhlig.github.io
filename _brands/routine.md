**Update (2026):** "Routine Natural Deodorant | Made in Canada", now under the name Routine Natural Beauty ([site](https://routinenaturalbeauty.com/)).

Made in Calgary, AB. Clay-based deodorants using natural fragrances and oils. They make ones with beeswax and without (the latter for vegans), and the usual deodorizing ingredient is baking soda (though they also make some without it, for sensitive skin).
