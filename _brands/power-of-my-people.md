**Update (2026):** still going, and still makes the majority of its range in Canada; check the product name or label, where pieces are marked "Made In Canada" ([site](https://powerofmypeople.com/)).

Made in Vancouver, BC. A line of casual to semi-formal garments for men and women. Their ethos is of a "not quite unisex" bent, with many of the women's styles borrowing heavily from menswear in terms of style and cut. They make a lot of button down shirts in a few different fits for men and women, as well as some tees, and accessories.
