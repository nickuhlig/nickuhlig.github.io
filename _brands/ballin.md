**Update (2026):** still family-run and calls itself "the leading North American manufacturer of men's pants and shorts", sold in over 1,000 stores ([site](https://ballin.com/)). The site doesn't say where it's made; directories list production in Québec (Montréal / St-Césaire) ([Made in Canada Directory](https://madeincanadadirectory.ca/?p=40094)).

Made in Montréal, QC. Makers of fine men's pants since 1946. Their website features an extensive [fashion dictionary](https://www.ballin.com/dictionary/) which is actually quite fascinating.
