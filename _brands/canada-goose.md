**Update (2026):** since 2019 Canada Goose has moved some of its production overseas, so not everything they sell is made in Canada any more.

Made in Toronto, ON and Winnipeg, MB. Founded in Toronto in 1957. Favourite of actual arctic explorers and trust fund brats with names like Becky and Chad the world over. Legendary warmth and quality, not much intro needed really. If you have $700+ to spend on a jacket, chances are you'll at least glance at one of these.
