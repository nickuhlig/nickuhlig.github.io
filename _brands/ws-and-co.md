**Update (2026):** still a 100% Canadian-owned Toronto factory. Its [Redwood Classics](https://redwoodclassics.net/) label is "proudly made and decorated in Canada" in that factory.

Made in Toronto, ON. Parent company of the Redwood Classics label, which consists primarily of tees and sweats. Their factory does private label work for many brands and has also produced the Olympic apparel for both Roots and Hudson's Bay Company.
