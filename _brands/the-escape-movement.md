**Update (2026):** the domain is for sale, and the brand appears to have closed.

Made in North Bay, ON. Streetwear consisting primarily of solid colours and simple patterns (like stripes or bisected two-colour garments), for men and women. The men's line consisted mostly of tees, tank tops, and sweatshirts; the women's line was similar, with a few accessories, leggings/pants, and leg warmers.
