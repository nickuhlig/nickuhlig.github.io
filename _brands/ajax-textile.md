**Update (2026):** Ajax Textile has permanently closed. It was a family-owned commission dye house (dyeing and finishing other companies' fabric) rather than a fabric mill, founded in 1954 ([profile](https://www.encircled.ca/blogs/live/get-to-know-one-of-the-last-remaining-dye-houses-in-canada)).

Made in Ajax, ON. Fabric mill in operation since 1953.
