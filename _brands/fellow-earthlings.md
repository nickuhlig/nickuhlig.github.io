**Update (2026):** no longer made in Canada. In January 2026 the Seggies relocated the business to George Town, Grand Cayman, where they still make their frames in-house in a combined workshop and store ([Cayman Compass](https://www.caymancompass.com/2026/01/30/husband-and-wife-team-bring-glasses-manufacturing-to-george-town/)). The old online shop is entirely sold out and hasn't been updated since early 2025.

Made in Guernsey Cove, PEI. A small bespoke eyewear company run by husband and wife duo Chris and Sydney Seggie, making acetate frames. They have also sold vintage deadstock leather sunglasses previously made by [Tannereye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tannereye).
