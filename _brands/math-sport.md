**Update (2026):** Math Sport later became YUL Run, which stopped operating a few years ago; the old domain is now an unrelated running-shoe blog.

Partially made in Saint-Léonard, QC. The only company I could find making running shoes in Canada, with a highly customizable shoe (colour, cushioning, and more) on Vibram soles.
