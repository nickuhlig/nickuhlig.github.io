**Update (2026):** Howard & Lloyd appears to have closed: its domain now belongs to a design studio, and the name is now used by a branding business.

Headquartered in Saskatoon, SK. Not sure where made. Limited-run streetwear with a fairly broad set of stylistic influences. Everything from plaid button downs to varsity jackets and vowel-less sans-serif logotype. Named after two farmers who allegedly signed a deal on the back of a pack of darts to purchase some land together in Saskatchewan.
