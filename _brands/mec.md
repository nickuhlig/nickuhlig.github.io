**Update (2026):** the co-op was sold to US private equity firm Kingswood Capital Management in 2020 and now runs as a for-profit company. Its own-label products are designed and tested in Vancouver but mostly made in Asia ([Wikipedia](https://Www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain_Equipment)).

Formerly Mountain Equipment Co-op, headquartered in Vancouver, BC. Legendary Canadian outdoor retailer with its own line of products alongside other brands, from ultralight camping gear to fuzzy socks. Only some items were ever made in Canada.
