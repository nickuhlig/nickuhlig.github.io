**Update (2026):** the brand is defunct; lowellmtl.ca now shows only a "page not found".

Made in Montréal, QC. Leather bags and accessories, made in a workshop in Hochelaga-Maisonneuve, with a flagship on St-Laurent in the Mile End. American leathers and sometimes recycled fur. Named after Jack Kerouac's hometown, symbolizing the bonds between Québécois and Americans.
