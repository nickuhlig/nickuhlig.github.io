**Update (2026):** defunct. Workshop was the in-house label of Vancouver menswear chain Boys'Co, which [shut down in 2021](https://retail-insider.com/retail-insider/2021/06/vancouver-based-menswear-retailer-boysco-shutting-down-operations-after-37-years/) after 37 years.

100% made in North America, some items made in Canada (probably in Vancouver, BC). Retail only, no online sales. In-house brand of Boys Co., a Vancouver-based clothing store. The line mostly consists of tees, crew-necks, and button-down shirts, while also featuring some sweats. Aesthetic bears resemblance to Outclass, Wings + Horns, and Bon Vivant.
