**Update (2026):** formerly Kania. The brand relaunched as Stacey Martin Lifestyle, Ottawa loungewear that press describes as ethically manufactured in Canada ([Shifter](https://shiftermagazine.com/fashion/kania-couture-stacey-martin-lifestyle), [Ottawa Business Journal](https://obj.ca/the-bright-side-of-business-fashion-industry-entrepreneur-stacey-martin-looks-to-scale-in-a-big-way)). The old kania.ca domain now belongs to an unrelated site.

Manufactured in multiple cities, designed in Ottawa, ON. Luxury sweatshirts for women. Everything from standard zip-up hoodies to varsity jacket-inspired designs and even a few dresses. Relatively simple design but with lots of bright colours and fabric patterns to offset the clean lines. Founded in 2003 by Stacey Martin Bafi-Yeboa; Kania means "light" in the Twi language of Ghana, West Africa.
