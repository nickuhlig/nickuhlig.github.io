**Update (2026):** "100% handcrafted" Goodyear-welt boots; the first company to produce cowboy boots in Canada ([site](https://bouletboots.com/)).

Made in Saint-Tite, QC. Established in 1933, Boulet is known primarily for western/cowboy boots, plus work boots, motorcycle boots, and styles for women and children.
