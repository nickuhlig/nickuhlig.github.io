**Update (2026):** NAQP's own line is still made in Canada.

Some items made in Toronto, ON, others elsewhere. NAQP is an online shop that has a lot of made in Canada and made in USA brands for sale, but they also have their own small line consisting of sweaters, button-downs, t-shirts, and some accessories like hats and gloves. The tees and sweats are made in Toronto by [WS & Co.](#brand-ws-and-co) (see the blanks & manufacturers section).
