**Update (2026):** "Fait à Montréal depuis 1998", and the atelier-boutique is still open ([site](https://www.kollontai.net/)).

Made in Montréal, QC. Founded in 1998 by Gabrielle Tousignant, the brand is named after Russian feminist political figure Alexandra Kollontai, who helped establish International Women's Day. Large variety of garments, from lingerie to outerwear. Patterns range from very minimalist to very dynamic, with many garments on the more muted side, featuring contrasting seams, buttons, or stitching. The in-stock range in their store is enormous (most likely spanning several seasons), so dive in.
