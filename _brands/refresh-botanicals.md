**Update (2026):** now based in Ponoka, AB, where its "research, development, and manufacturing processes all converge under one roof" ([site](https://www.refreshbotanicals.com/pages/about-us)).

Formerly headquartered in Calgary, AB; formulated in Québec, and manufactured in Barrie, ON. Owned by parent company Taiga BioActives, who also make [MAXeffects Naturals](#brand-maxeffects-naturals). Natural botanical skin care products, such as toners, cleansers, and makeup removers.
