**Update (2026):** still operating through [her site](https://www.jordanderuiter.com/) and an Etsy shop, and still made in Canada.

Made in Toronto, ON. Eponymous women's wear and lingerie brand founded by a George Brown College School of Fashion graduate. Designs span a huge range, covering everything from bridal & bridesmaids dresses to rompers to lingerie. Other than the bridal wear, many garments feature lots of colour and eye-catching patterns.
