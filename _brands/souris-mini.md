**Update (2026):** long made in Québec, Souris Mini moved its production to China and India and kept only design in Québec ([McCord Stewart Museum](https://encyclomodeqc.musee-mccord-stewart.ca/en/entry/souris-mini/)). Its site still leans on being made with love in Québec, while retail listings show products designed in Québec and made in China ([Costco](https://www.costco.ca/p/-/souris-mini-boys-and-girls-2-piece-set/4000445847?langId=-25)).

Children's clothing.
