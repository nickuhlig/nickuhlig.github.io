**Update (2026):** coKANna appears to be defunct; its store is gone and there's no other trace of it.

Made in Scarborough (Toronto), ON. Clothing designed for women who are living with cancer. The impetus for design came from the owner's experience after her cancer diagnosis, when she found a lack of adaptive women's clothing in the market. Clothes are designed to be easy to put on for people with limited mobility and soft on the skin. Everything from jackets to head scarves.
