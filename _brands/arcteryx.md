**Update (2026):** foreign-owned and outsourcing more and more, despite still making a small share in Canada. Arc'teryx was bought in 2019 with the rest of Amer Sports by an investor group led by China's Anta Sports. It says it makes only about 5% of its product in Canada, at its own ARC'One facility in New Westminster; the majority is made in the Asia-Pacific region ([ARC'One](https://arcteryx.com/explore/supply-chain-partners/arc-one), [manufacturing partners](https://arcteryx.com/ca/en/product-advice/arcteryx-manufacturing-partners)).

North Vancouver-founded maker of technical outdoor gear and outerwear. Its urban line is [Veilance](#brand-arcteryx-veilance).
