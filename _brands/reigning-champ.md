**Update (2026):** acquired by Aritzia in 2021, and as of 2025 Reigning Champ has moved its manufacturing out of Canada ([Made in CA](https://madeinca.ca/?p=1954)). Its Vancouver maker, [CYC](#brand-cyc-design-corporation), has since closed.

Formerly made in Vancouver, BC, by the same company as [Wings + Horns](#brand-wings-and-horns). Primarily athletic wear (including boxing robes), plus jackets/outerwear and hats. Known for the exceptionally high quality of its fleece.
