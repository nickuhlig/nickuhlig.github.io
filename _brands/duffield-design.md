**Update (2026):** now in Almonte: "small batch manufacturing is alive and well at our studio-boutique in historic Almonte, Ontario" ([site](https://duffielddesign.ca/)).

Made in Dunrobin, ON (technically part of Ottawa, ON). All designs made by Megan Duffield, using eco-friendly fabrics, including many sourced from within Canada. The brand also places a significant general emphasis on sustainability. Casual wear from dresses to tops and leggings. Most pieces seem to focus on freedom of movement and comfort.
