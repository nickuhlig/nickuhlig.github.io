**Update (2026):** "Manufacturing Excellence since 1915"; about half of its products say made in Canada, while much of the rest is Italian leather goods ([site](https://www.stolenriches.com/)).

Made in Toronto, ON. Shoelaces for fancily-dressed gentlefolk. They've been quietly making shoelaces since 1915, but only recently expanded into the modern look they now sport. They also make playful lapel pins, bracelets, and pocket squares in coordinated colour sets.
