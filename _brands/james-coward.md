Vancouver menswear label founded in 2014 by Daniel Garrod and Joseph Walia, mixing understated tailoring with workwear. Clothing is marked as made in Vancouver or elsewhere in Canada; some items, including footwear, come from the UK and Japan ([site](https://www.james-coward.com/)).
