**Update (2026):** "Ethically Made in Canada", "Made in Ottawa" ([site](https://shop.chayle.ca/)).

Made in Ottawa, ON. Jewellery primarily for women, but some unisex designs can be found. Chayle places special emphasis on using ethical materials, including fair trade gemstones, Canadian diamonds, and metals that are predominantly recycled. Founded by Chayle Cook in 2013.
