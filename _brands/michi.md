**Update (2026):** the brand cites "Made in Canada manufacturing" in Toronto as one of its strengths; fabrics include Italian mesh ([About](https://michiny.com/pages/about)).

Made in Toronto, ON. Women's activewear founded in 2010 by Michelle Watson, who previously designed activewear for Ralph Lauren. Ostensibly activewear, but with such a high-fashion influence that it looks like fancy dress. Very unusual cuts and silhouettes, always focused on freedom of movement and staying cool. Everything from underwear (where they got their start) to jackets.
