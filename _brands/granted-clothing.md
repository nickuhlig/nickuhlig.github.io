**Update (2026):** Granted [closed in 2025](https://www.richmond-news.com/local-news/clothing-company-granted-closing-handmade-knit-sweaters-vancouver-canada-2025-10214132), after nearly 50 years; owner Brian Hirano cited the difficulty of finding knitters and rising costs.

Made in Richmond, BC. Beautiful hand-knit wool sweaters and accessories with lots of interesting patterns (including things like owls and CBC logos).
