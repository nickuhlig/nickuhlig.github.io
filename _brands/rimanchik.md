**Update (2026):** "artisan-designed and made in Montreal, Quebec" ([site](https://rimanchik.com/)).

Made in Montréal, QC. A wide variety of products including bags, watches, belts, wallets, and other accessories. Most feature leather as their central material of construction. Founded in 2003, Rimanchik is the brainchild of Sandra Singh and Victor Nolivos. The name means "we & you speak" in the Quichua language.
