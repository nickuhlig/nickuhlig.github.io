**Update (2026):** "Handcrafted in East Vancouver since 1946", with a limited lifetime warranty ([site](https://daytonboots.com/)).

Made in Vancouver, BC. Founded in 1946, with a reputation for boots often involved in fights thanks to the bellicose nature of their owners (loggers, motorcyclists, and shitkicker Canadians), which got them banned in some establishments. Known more recently for a pre-distressed service boot collaboration with [Wings + Horns](#brand-wings-and-horns). Made-to-order is their bread and butter.
