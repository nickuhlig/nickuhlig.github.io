**Update (2026):** dutil's own-label pieces are now made in Canada: their jeans are listed as "Designed and manufactured in Canada" ([example](https://dutildenim.com/collections/dutil/products/dutil-q-slim-taper-black-overdye)). The store also sells many other denim brands, most of them not Canadian-made.

*2019:* Made in California, USA. Denim shop right next to Sydney's on Queen Street in downtown Toronto. They make some of their own items. Their denim fabric used to come from Cone Mills in Greensboro, North Carolina until that factory went under in 2017. The jeans are sewn in California. One of two exceptions to the "Made in Canada" rule on this list.
