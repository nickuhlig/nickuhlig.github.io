**Update (2026):** its own rain gear is listed as made in Canada ([site](https://fairechild.com/)). The shop also sells some imported extras, such as merino base layers made in Germany, socks made in China and Japan, and sweaters made in Peru.

Made in Dartmouth, NS. Founded by Tabitha Osler, Faire Child makes outerwear (more specifically rain gear) for children. All garments are made from recycled PET (rPET), and include design features that allow them to "grow with the child" so that they will fit for up to 3 years.
