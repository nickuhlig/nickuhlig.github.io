**Update (2026):** still "proudly crafted in Canada", making made-to-measure and bespoke dress shirts plus women's and sleepwear private label ([site](https://shirtfit.com/)).

Made in Concord, ON. Parent company of Shirtfit and Royal Shirt Co. Founded in 1969 by Italian immigrants Luigi and Maria d'Abbondanza, and now helmed by their daughter Anita. Yet another manufacturer that was tapped by Raised by Wolves for some of their elongated flannel button-downs in the Fall/Winter 2015 release. Shirtfit has also made garments for Harry Rosen and Barney's New York. Royal Shirt Co. is stocked by Model Citizen in Toronto's Kensington Market neighbourhood, the only retailer in Canada that I know of for this brand.
