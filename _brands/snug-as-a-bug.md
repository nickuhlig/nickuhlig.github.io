**Update (2026):** the online shop now shows "This store is unavailable", and the brand appears to have closed. A few retailers still list leftover stock.

Made in Toronto, ON. Pyjamas for adults and children. They specialized in onesies, but also made two-piece pyjama sets and some items for babies and pets. Patterns ranged from truly wacky ones to more classic things like buffalo check, snowflake designs, and various types of tartan/plaid.
