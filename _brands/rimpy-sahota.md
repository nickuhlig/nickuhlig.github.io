**Update (2026):** the brand was bought by private equity and no longer appears to exist; its site no longer loads. It was made in Vancouver from Canadian-made fabrics ([Daily Hive](https://dailyhive.com/vancouver/rimpy-sahota-true-made-canada-fashion-designer)).

Made in Vancouver, BC. Everyday women's wear with a fashion-forward tilt. Playful patterns and bright colours much in evidence. Most garments used fabrics that were also made in Canada.
