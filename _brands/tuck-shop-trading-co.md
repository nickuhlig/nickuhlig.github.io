**Update (2026):** the online shop has been locked behind a "reopening soon" page for years, and the brand appears to have closed.

Made in several locations, primarily Montréal, QC and Toronto, ON. Known primarily for their "city of neighbourhoods" line, featuring hats with the names of Canadian neighbourhoods on them (e.g. Mile-End, Roncesvalles), later with other cities' neighbourhoods (NYC, LA, etc.) as well.
