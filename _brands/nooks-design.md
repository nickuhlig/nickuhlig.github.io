**Update (2026):** since 2017 most of the line has been made by Purnaa, a fair-trade manufacturer in Kathmandu, Nepal. Only a few items, such as the upcycled cashmere hats, are still handmade in Kelowna ([site](https://nooksdesign.ca/pages/about-us)).

Made in Kelowna, BC. Originally making only children's footwear, this family company now creates a range of children's apparel and footwear (this varies from season to season). Items were made from re-used or upcycled materials, typically wool and vintage leather. The name derives from the nickname of the creators' daughter.
