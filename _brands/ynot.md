**Update (2026):** "cycling accessories Made in Canada" ([site](https://ynotmade.com/)).

Made in Toronto, ON. Established in 2009 by Tony Mammoliti, YNOT make a wide range of cycling-oriented products. Backpacks, messenger bags, panniers, pedal straps, tuques, 5-panels, and accessories. They've recently expanded into apparel as well, and they also make nice duffel bags.
