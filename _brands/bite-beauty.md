**Update (2026):** a sad story. Bite was sold to LVMH's beauty incubator Kendo in 2014, and the new owner reformulated many of the products, which customers did not like. Founder Susanne Langmuir left in 2018, and Kendo closed the brand in 2022. Its custom-lipstick shops carry on as [Lip Lab](#brand-lip-lab) ([FashionNetwork](https://us.fashionnetwork.com/news/Canada-s-Bite-Beauty-closes-shop-after-a-decade-of-business,1407070.html), [Beauty Independent](https://www.beautyindependent.com/kendo-bite-beauty-closing-lip-lab-stores-arent-going-away/)).

Made in Toronto, ON. Lipsticks, glosses, and makeup, made with food-grade ingredients. The factory was on Geary Ave., an indescribably ugly little street in Dovercourt Village, Toronto that was experiencing some hype and gentrification. Bite also had a "lab" in SoHo, NYC where custom colours could be blended on the spot.
