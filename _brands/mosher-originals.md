Made in Toronto, ON. Founded in 2012, the company got its start in making coats for dogs, strangely enough. From there they've expanded into making backpacks and accessories for humans as well. Designs range from courier-friendly backpacks to belt bags and day packs for hikers, to backpacks for kids.
