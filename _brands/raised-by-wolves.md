**Update (2026):** increasingly outsourcing production. Many items in its shop are still marked "Made in Canada" ([site](https://raisedbywolves.ca/)), but recent coverage says the brand now "primarily makes in Egypt" ([Nuvo](https://nuvomagazine.com/daily-edit/5-canadian-streetwear-brands-to-freshen-up-your-wardrobe)).

Headquartered in Montréal, QC (formerly Ottawa, ON), with garments made by various manufacturers, in 2019 almost always in Canada. Very popular with the streetwear crowd, sometimes half-seriously called the Canadian Supreme. The line consists entirely of single-season releases, so every product is essentially limited edition. A mixture of in-your-face branding and more subtle pieces, plus collaborations with brands like Ebbets Field Flannels, Vans, and Filling Pieces. Broad mix of apparel and accessories, even socks and incense.
