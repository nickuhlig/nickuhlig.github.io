**Update (2026):** still operating ([site](https://empireclothing.com/)). The site makes no made-in claim, but directories say it still manufactures in Montréal using fabrics from Italy, England, Ireland and Scotland ([Made in Canada Directory](https://madeincanadadirectory.ca/item/empire-clothing/)). Not to be confused with [Chemise Empire](#brand-chemise-empire), the Louiseville shirt maker.

Made in Montréal, QC. Founded in 1917 by Romanian immigrant Joseph Leibovitch, Empire makes men's dress attire for the Empire, Biella, and Peter Millar labels, primarily distributed in North America. They also do made to measure.
