**Update (2026):** a decade of handcrafted footwear and goods from its East Vancouver studio ([site](https://www.lovejulesleather.com/)).

Made in Vancouver, BC. Founded around 2013 on the back of a Kickstarter campaign by Julia Vagelatos and Joshua Blodans. Beautiful and ruinously expensive footwear in limited runs, with rubber soles made in-house.
