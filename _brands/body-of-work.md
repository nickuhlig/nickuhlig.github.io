Toronto sportswear label founded in 2020 by Dwayne Vatcher and Brittney MacKinnon. Its garments are made in Canada with family-owned fabric mills and factories ([site](https://bodyofwork-studio.com/pages/made-in-canada)). Footwear collaborations are made by its partners outside Canada.
