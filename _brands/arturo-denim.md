**Update (2026):** Arturo Denim went out of business in 2024. Until then its jeans were made from Japanese denim, mostly sewn in Saint-Côme-Linière in the Beauce, with a store and workshop on 124 Street in Edmonton ([CBC](https://amp.cbc.ca/news/canada/edmonton/arturo-denim-ethical-fashion-jeans-edmonton-1.4824475)).

Made in Beauce, QC and Edmonton, AB. A recently-founded Edmonton-based label that makes jeans for men and women. The majority of their pants are made by the same factory that produces Second Jeans (see below), but they also make a small portion of their line in their Edmonton studio on 124th street.
