**Update (2026):** still Sydney's in-house label, sold through [Sydney's](https://shopsydneys.com/collections/united-stock-dry-goods) in Toronto. Made in Canada from Japanese and European fabrics, except knitwear and footwear ([Made in CA](https://madeinca.ca/?p=2449)).

Made in Toronto, ON. Company started by the owner of Sydney's, a menswear shop located on Queen Street West in downtown Toronto. Raw denim is the focus, but they also make menswear. They have a single fit of jean (ultra tapered) in several colours, as well as making sweats, tees, and button downs. They also have a limited range of footwear.
