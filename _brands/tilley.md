**Update (2026):** after founder Alex Tilley sold the company to UK private equity firm Hilco in 2015 (it was sold again in 2018, to Toronto's Gibraltar & Company), production has moved largely overseas, to China, Portugal, Italy, and Japan. The site still has a "Made in Canada" section, but only a small fraction of products say made in Canada ([Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/2112610/tilley-endurables-sold-to-uk-company-will-production-stay-in-canada), [Globe and Mail](https://theglobeandmail.com/business/article-joe-mimran-tilley-hat/)).

Headquartered in Toronto, ON. Known primarily for their hats, they also make shirts, underwear, and other accessories. As of 2019 they stated that "everything with their name on it is made in Canada", with the only exception being one style of sock, made in Iowa.
