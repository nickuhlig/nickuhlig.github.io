**Update (2026):** made "partly in our own Factory and HQ space in Victoria, BC" and partly with partner factories in Vancouver, Ontario, Quebec and New York ([their factory page](https://ecologyst.com/pages/the-factory)).

Not sure where in Canada they're made, but the company is based in Victoria, BC. They were called Sitka until 2019 when they underwent a name change to distinguish themselves from the American company. ecologyst makes a wide range of apparel for men and women, from button down shirts to sweats to outerwear to a small selection of denim. They've begun placing a big focus on using natural materials and avoiding petroleum products. They also make surfboards.
