**Update (2026):** acquired in 2017 by Montréal's Luxury Men's Apparel Group (LMAG), owner of [Samuelsohn](#brand-samuelsohn) and Hickey Freeman, which said Lipson would continue to manufacture dress shirts in its Toronto facility ([FashionNetwork](https://us.fashionnetwork.com/news/Lmag-acquires-lipson-shirtmakers,804652.html)). It still operates; lipsonshirtmakers.com now redirects to samuelsohn.com.

Made in Toronto, ON. A wide range of dress shirts and other button-down styles in numerous cuts and fabrics. Founded in 1958 and featuring rather distinct branding that involves a replica of Alexandre Dumas' sword.
