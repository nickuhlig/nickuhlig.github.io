**Update (2026):** still cutting and sewing in its Truro mill, but the site now says only "Many products made in Canada", and it has been outsourcing more and more ([site](https://www.stanfields.com/)).

Made in Truro, NS. Founded in Truro in 1870 as Truro Woolen Mills. Interestingly, they made a particular long underwear "unshrinkable" onesie that was popular with Klondike miners in the gold rush of 1897. Now they make a range of underwear, socks, and some basics like t-shirts. For men, women, and children.
