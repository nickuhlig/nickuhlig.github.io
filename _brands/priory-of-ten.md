**Update (2026):** the brand is defunct; its site is now an empty shell. It was made in a family-run factory in Vancouver's garment district ([Made in CA](https://madeinca.ca/?p=3541)).

Austere and elegant women's clothing in solid colours, very minimalist in appearance. Similar in some ways to [Ovate](#brand-ovate). The brand began as an in-house label for the Vancouver clothing store Board of Trade, owned by Eunice Quan and David Lin. The founding designer, Mei Liu, is a Chinese-Canadian double emigrée who later lived in Toronto and then New York.
