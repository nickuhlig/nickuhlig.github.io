**Update (2026):** still listed by Frame Barn in Toronto as "acetate frames hand crafted to look like wood", though the site doesn't say outright where they're made ([site](https://www.frame-barn.com/woody)).

Made in Toronto, ON. Woody is a daughter brand of Frame Barn, with manufacturing all done in Toronto. Both prescription and sunglass frames are made of cellulose acetate (a plant-sourced material) but textured and coloured to look like wood.
