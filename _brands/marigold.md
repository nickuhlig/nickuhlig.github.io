**Update (2026):** "Fait à Montréal pour de vrai" ([site](https://marigoldmtl.com/)).

Made in Montréal, QC. Modern women's clothing, featuring interesting patterns (some very cubist-looking) and an almost Japanese aesthetic in some of their garments. Dresses, sweats, skirts, and blouses. Founded in 2014 by Marilyn Baril.
