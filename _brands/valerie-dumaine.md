**Update (2026):** Valérie Dumaine died of cancer in February 2025, aged 47. The label had moved to Ontario from 2019 (Toronto, then Kingston, then Guelph), and its old shop domain now hosts spam ([McCord Stewart Museum](https://encyclomodeqc.musee-mccord-stewart.ca/en/entry/valerie-dumaine/)).

Made in Montréal, QC. Simple and androgynous-leaning women's fashions. Straddling the line between casual and formal, with some similarities to brands like Jennifer Glasgow and Eve Gravel. Many of the tops, dresses, and jumpsuits were looser-fitting and boxier in construction. Founded in 2004.
