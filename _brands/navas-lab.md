**Update (2026):** Navas Lab is defunct; its online store is offline.

Made in Vancouver, BC. The spawn of another Kickstarter, Navas makes bamboo-fabric clothing for slim, tall men. All but one of their items are manufactured in Vancouver. So far the product line consists of tees, sweats, and a couple of water resistant jackets. The one item made overseas is a pair of boardshorts.
