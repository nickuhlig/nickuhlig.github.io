**Update (2026):** Muttonhead now goes by MTN.HEAD, with stores in Toronto and Collingwood. Its site is titled "Made in Canada" and says its own pieces are "made close to home"; it also stocks other Canadian brands ([site](https://www.mtnhead.com/)).

Made in Toronto, ON for the most part. Lots of sweats, tees, hats, dress shirts, and slacks, with a more active, outdoorsy slant. All their clothes are designed to be unisex, and most are entirely manufactured in Toronto. Some are made in the USA (hats and baseball shirts, for instance). Recent lines have begun to incorporate more technical fabrics, like their waterproof camping hoodies and some of their jackets. Founded by Meg Sinclair, the brand was originally conceived as a thesis collection for her program at Ryerson University School of Fashion.
