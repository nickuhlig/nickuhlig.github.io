**Update (2026):** the online shop at getyourbo.com is closed, and the last press coverage is from 2014–15, and the brand appears to have closed.

Made from reclaimed materials in Vancouver, BC. Wooden bowties, founded by father and son Mehran and Sam Mansouri. Wide selection of styles with colour accents to allow matching and coordinating, including a tuxedo-appropriate "black" line and a line made of concrete from a collaboration with [dconstruct](#brand-dconstruct).
