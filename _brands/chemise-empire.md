**Update (2026):** Montréal menswear brand Au Noir [bought Chemise Empire in 2019](https://aunoir.shop/en/pages/copy-of-a-propos), to make its shirts locally. Empire's own website is gone.

Made in Louiseville, QC. Uniform, dress, and military shirt makers. Raised by Wolves used their services for oxford cloth button downs in one season a few years back.
