**Update (2026):** Betina Lou's own line is now small; its shop mostly carries other labels (not all Canadian-made), but what it still makes itself is made in Quebec. Its menswear house brand is [Marmier](#brand-marmier).

Made in Montréal, QC. Most garments have a clean, minimalist look, with some items (dresses primarily) incorporating louder patterns. Dresses, jeans, tops and skirts, along with a plethora of other products (not all of which are made by them). They also feature accessories and jewelry from a number of designers, both local and international, and their online store stocks some made-in-Montréal menswear such as brother label Marmier.
