**Update (2026):** "Most of our products are made in Canada"; a few come from specialist partners in Germany, Italy, and the Czech Republic, and the brushes from China ([site](https://vasanticosmetics.com/pages/faqs)).

Made in Scarborough, ON (but based in Markham). Wide range of makeup and skin care products for women. Founded in 1998 by three sisters as a response to the general lack of makeup options for non-Caucasian women in North America, especially those of south-Asian descent.
