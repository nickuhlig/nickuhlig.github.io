**Update (2026):** the artvictus.com domain is parked, and I couldn't find any current trace of the business.

Headquartered in Mississauga, ON, with garments made in multiple locations in Canada. Custom-made dress shirts with lots of colour and fabric options, at relatively reasonable prices. Their staple was the Oxford, but they would make just about anything (the one unfortunate exception being dress shirts with Mandarin/banded collars).
