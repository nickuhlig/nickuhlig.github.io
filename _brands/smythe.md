**Update (2026):** now at [shopsmythe.com](https://shopsmythe.com/), still an independent Toronto business, with its first boutique opened in 2022. The site makes no made-in claim, but retailers list most styles as made in Canada, with a few made in Portugal and some knits by artisans in Bolivia ([Revolve](https://www.revolve.com/ca/v/smythe-blazers), [Made in CA](https://madeinca.ca/?p=5237)).

Based in Toronto, ON. Apparel for women, with a somewhat more professional and/or high-fashion slant. The brand is primarily known for their blazers, which feature interesting cuts and patterns.
