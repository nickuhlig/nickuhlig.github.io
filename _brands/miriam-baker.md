**Update (2026):** the site has said "down for maintenance / opening soon" for some time, and the brand appears to have closed. It opened a Bayview Village boutique in 2021 and sewed in its Toronto studio ([Streets of Toronto](https://streetsoftoronto.com/toronto-culture/toronto-designers-first-boutique-aims-to-bring-greater-size-inclusivity-to-womens-fashion/)).

Made in Toronto, ON. Formalwear for women with larger busts. As the focus implies, they sell a lot of tops and dresses, but they also do bottoms to facilitate matching outfits. The company was founded by and is named after a Ryerson School of Fashion graduate.
