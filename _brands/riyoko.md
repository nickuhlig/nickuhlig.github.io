**Update (2026):** still running from its Calgary studio (moving to NVRLND in Ramsay in spring 2026). Made entirely in North America, in Canada and the United States ([site](https://riyoko.ca/)).

Made in Canada & the United States. Originally made in the founder's studio in Calgary, and still headquartered there. Riyoko began as a clothing line for cyclists, but has expanded to cater to nearly every kind of active woman: yoga, hiking, maternity wear, and so on.
