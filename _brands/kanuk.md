**Update (2026):** still designed and made "in a small factory in the heart of Montreal" ([Made in Canada](https://www.kanuk.com/en_us/made-in-canada)). A Montréal investor group led by Bertrand Cesvet took control in 2024 ([Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240507949482/en/Kanuk-Changes-Hands)).

Made in Montréal, QC. Founded in the 1970s, Kanuk makes winter coats and warm accessories (scarves, hats), plus lighter seasonal outerwear.
