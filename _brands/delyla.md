**Update (2026):** Delyla has closed; its website domain is now for sale.

Made in Montréal, QC. Fabric mill in operation since 2002. They also make some of their own items, including terry cloth robes and a few different styles of t-shirt. Heavy focus on eco-fabrics such as hemp, bamboo, recycled polyester, recycled cotton, and organic cotton.
