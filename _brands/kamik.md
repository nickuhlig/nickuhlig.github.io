**Update (2026):** Kamik says 70% of its products are still made in North America, at factories in Québec, Ontario, and New Hampshire ([Made in CA](https://madeinca.ca/?p=1138)).

Made primarily in Montréal, QC. Footwear company founded in 1898 making winter boots, rubber boots, safety shoes, and more, with a recycling program for old boots.
