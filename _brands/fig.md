**Update (2026):** FIG's site now only says its clothes are "designed in Montréal". By 2018 about a fifth of its production was in Shanghai and Taiwan ([McCord Museum](https://encyclomodeqc.musee-mccord-stewart.ca/en/?p=2102)), and it now has a separate "Made in Canada" collection, made in Quebec and Ontario.

Made in Montréal, QC. Described as "travel wear for women". The theme of their garments is comfort and ease of movement. They also make some accessories. FIG also makes an effort not only to manufacture here, but to source fabrics from Canada as well, and they frequently make use of organic and eco-friendly fabrics in their pieces.
