**Update (2026):** still going (FW26), but its site doesn't say where the clothes are made; a directory lists it as made in Toronto ([Made in CA](https://madeinca.ca/?p=5194)). Its jewellery is made locally from recycled materials.

Probably made in Toronto, ON. Originally started as a jewelry line, Beaufille now encompasses a ready to wear line as well. The name (an intentionally incorrect combination of the masculine "beau" and feminine "fille") and the image focus on a more androgynous expression of femininity.
