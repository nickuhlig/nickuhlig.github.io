**Update (2026):** the domain now belongs to an unrelated gambling site, and the brand appears to have closed.

Made in Montréal, QC. Originally a maker of triangular "baby bandanas" which incorporated an attached pacifier clip, ElectrikKidz went on to make a range of products for children of ages 0-5 from outerwear to sleep sacks. Their products featured fun and often hilarious designs, from David Bowie to Montréal Expos memorabilia.
