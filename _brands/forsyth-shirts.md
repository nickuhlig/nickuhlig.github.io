**Update (2026):** the brand still operates, but its shirts are now made overseas. The Cambridge factory closed in 2013 after 110 years of production, when the company sought bankruptcy protection ([CTV](https://kitchener.ctvnews.ca/100-out-of-work-as-cambridge-shirt-manufacturer-announces-closure-1.1173150), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Forsyth_(clothier))).

Formerly made in Cambridge, ON. Founded in 1903. Their main business is the Oxford shirt in its many varieties.
