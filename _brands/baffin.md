**Update (2026):** several boot lines are made at Baffin's own Stoney Creek factory and sold in "Made in Canada" collections; other products are imported ([site](https://www.baffin.com/collections/outdoor-footwear-made-in-canada)).

Some products made in Stoney Creek (Hamilton), ON. Founded in 1997, Baffin makes outerwear and apparel for extreme conditions, including jackets, boots, and thermal layers such as socks and underwear, plus industrial products like CSA safety footwear.
