**Update (2026):** "Raber Glove products are made in Manitoba and worn worldwide" ([site](https://raberglove.com/)).

Made in Winnipeg, MB. Leather gloves and mittens for all sorts of applications. Founded in 1941 by Abraham Raber, the company supplies many police and military agencies with gloves for use in the field. They also make dress gloves, arctic mittens, and work gloves for us civilians.
