Sunglasses cut from retired skateboard decks. "Every pair of sunglasses is cut and finished by me, one board at a time, in my workshop in Canada". Since December 2017 the frames have been made in Ottawa "start to finish", on the maker's own CNC router and laser engraver and finished by hand ([site](https://skrp.ca/our-story/)).

Made in Ottawa, ON. Started by Chris, a web developer turned woodworker. Skate shops across Canada and the US send in their retired decks, and buyers pick which section of a board (and its original graphic) becomes their pair. Each board stays on sale for 14 days, and if every spot sells, everyone gets 20% back.
