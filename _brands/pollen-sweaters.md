**Update (2026):** still knitting its sweaters in Lund, BC: "No offshore sweatshops. Ours is here at home." ([site](https://pollensweaters.com/))

Made in Powell River, BC. Simple, no-nonsense woolen sweaters for adults and children. They use a special type of wool that is non-itchy and machine washable for easy care. Their garments come in an almost ludicrous array of colours, and consist mostly of crew- and V-neck sweaters as well as a few hats and accessories.
