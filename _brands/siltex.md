**Update (2026):** the original Winnipeg mill is now Siltex's head office. Its "Bare Knits" line is still made in Canada, but it also sources fabric through a "network around the world", and [shipping records](https://panjiva.com/Siltex-Mills/2136227) show imports from Taiwan and China ([about Siltex](https://www.siltex.com/about-us/)).

Made in Winnipeg, MB. Fabric mill in operation since 1970.
