**Update (2026):** "Canadian-Made High End Lingerie and Loungewear"; most product pages say made in Canada, though a few pieces are made in Latvia ([site](https://lessisamor.com/)).

Made in Toronto, ON. Founded by Russian émigrée Irina Shad, Less is A'mor produces women's intimates with unconventional designs. Pieces are often quite ornate. They also make swimwear inspired by the lingerie designs.
