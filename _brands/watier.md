**Update (2026):** formerly Lise Watier, acquired by [Marcelle](#brand-marcelle). "Many are also manufactured and distributed right here in Canada" ([site](https://www.lisewatier.com/)).

Made in Montréal, QC. Lise Watier is the oldest originally Canadian makeup brand, founded in 1972. The brand tries to use Canadian ingredients when possible (for instance, Labrador tea, which contains high levels of vitamin C).
