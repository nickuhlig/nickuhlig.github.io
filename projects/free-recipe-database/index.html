Recipe Export

Pick your filters and format, then export -- everything happens in this browser tab; the recipe files are downloaded from this site and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Menu (exports one recipe per family -- the closest match to your choice, see below)

A family with several diet-swap versions of the same recipe (Classic, Vegan, a gluten-free version, ...) only ever exports ONE of them — the one that matches your choice here, avoids everything checked below, and (if more than one still qualifies) is explicitly labeled for your choice, then closest to the original. Want more than one menu? Export them in separate runs. A family with no recipe matching your choice (and avoiding everything checked below) is excluded entirely.

Dietary restrictions to avoid (checking any drops, before the Menu pick above is made, any candidate that violates one or more of the checked restrictions — unknown restrictions are treated as violating; a family left with nothing that satisfies both Menu and these is excluded entirely)
Formats

Norish scales servings and converts units itself, which is why units and servings above are single-choice. A recipe whose family has nothing at all at the serving size you picked is still included, at whichever size it does offer (see Menu above) -- true for every format, not just Norish.
